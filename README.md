# LuckyBlock TACZ
**TACZ 枪械盲盒幸运方块**

**LuckyBlock TACZ** 是一款专为 **TACZ（永恒与经典零）** 及其衍生战术模组打造的轻量级附属。
厌倦了在整合包里慢慢合成枪械？现在，你只需要一把镐子，就能体验极致的“军火盲盒”！

---

## 核心特性 (Features)

*   **绝对精准匹配 (Perfectly Matched)**：纯底层代码驱动，随机掉落枪械时会**100% 附带其对应的专属子弹**（默认 60 发）。绝对不会出现“拿到 AK47 却给了手枪子弹”的尴尬情况。
*   **超丰富掉落池 (Massive Loot Pool)**：默认内置 **86 种**随机掉落物！包含：
    *   TACZ 原版及扩展的 **全部 70+ 把枪械**（步枪、狙击枪、霰弹枪、手枪、RPG）。
    *   **DeltaForce 三角洲行动** 近战刀皮（如：北极星）。
    *   **LRTactical 绿葡萄战术装备**（如：闪光弹）。
*   **即捡即用 (Ready to Fire)**：物品直接以实体形式弹出，捡起后直接开火，无缝接入战斗。
*   **极其轻量 (Lightweight)**：不添加任何多余的物品或方块，对整合包极其友好，适合随时加入生存档。

---

## 前置要求 (Dependencies)

为了正常运行，你必须安装以下模组：
*   **[TACZ (Timeless and Classics Zero)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/timeless-and-classics-zero)** (必需, Forge 1.20.1)
*   **[LRTactical (绿葡萄战术装备)](https://modrinth.com/mod/lr-tactical)** (必需, Forge 1.20.1)
*   **[DeltaForce Melee Pack (三角洲刀皮)](https://modrinth.com/mod/deltaforce-melee-pack)** (必需, Forge 1.20.1)

---

## 如何使用 (How to Use)

1. 在生存模式或创造模式下获得幸运方块：`/give @p luckyblocktacz:lucky_block_tacz`
2. 放置并破坏它。
3. 享受你的军火盲盒！

---

## 注意事项 (Notes)

*   本模组为**非官方** TACZ 附属，代码逻辑基于 Forge 1.20.1 编写。
*   如果你的整合包里还有其他第三方 TACZ 枪包，它们**不会**自动加入掉落池（掉落池基于当前扫描的 JSON 生成）。

# LuckyBlock TACZ
**TACZ Gun Blind Box Lucky Block **LuckyBlock TACZ** is a lightweight addon designed specifically for **TACZ (Timeless and Classics Zero)** and its derivative tactical mods**

Tired of slowly crafting guns in modpacks? Now, all you need is a pickaxe to experience the ultimate “arms blind box”!  

---  

## Core Features  
* **Perfectly Matched**: Driven purely by underlying code, whenever a gun drops, it will **100% come with its corresponding ammo** (default: 60 rounds). No more awkward moments of getting an AK47 but receiving pistol bullets.  
* **Massive Loot Pool**: Comes with **86 types** of random drops by default, including:  
  * All **70 guns** from the TACZ base game and expansions (rifles, sniper rifles, shotguns, pistols, RPGs).  
  * **DeltaForce Melee Skins** (like the Polaris knife).  
  * **LRTactical Green Grape Tactical Gear** (like flashbangs).  
* **Ready to Fire**: Items spawn as entities and can be used immediately upon pickup, seamlessly fitting into combat.  
* **Lightweight**: Adds no unnecessary items or blocks, very modpack-friendly, perfect for adding to your survival world anytime.  

---  

## Dependencies  
To run properly, you must have the following mods installed:  
* **[TACZ (Timeless and Classics Zero)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/timeless-and-classics-zero)** (Required, Forge 1.20.1)  
* **[LRTactical (Green Grape Tactical Gear)](https://modrinth.com/mod/lr-tactical)** (Required, Forge 1.20.1)  
* **[DeltaForce Melee Pack (Delta Knife Skins)](https://modrinth.com/mod/deltaforce-melee-pack)** (Required, Forge 1.20.1)  

---

## How to Use

1. Get a Lucky Block in Survival or Creative mode: `/give @p luckyblocktacz:lucky_block_tacz`
2. Place it and break it.
3. Enjoy your arms loot box!

---

## Notes

* This mod is an **unofficial** TACZ add-on, with code logic based on Forge 1.20.1.
* If your modpack also has other third-party TACZ gun packs, they **will not** be automatically added to the drop pool (the drop pool is generated based on currently scanned JSON).
